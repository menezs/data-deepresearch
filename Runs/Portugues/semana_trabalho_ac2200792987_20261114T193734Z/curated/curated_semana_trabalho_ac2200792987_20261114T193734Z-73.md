## Is there a 4-day week in Brazil?

No. Brazil has no 4-day week law, but Congress promulgated Constitutional Amendment 141 on 16 October 2026. It will end the 6x1 schedule (six working days, one rest day) by guaranteeing two paid rest days a week, and cuts the maximum week from 44 to 40 hours in two steps. That is a five-day, 40-hour week, not a four-day one.

The 40-hour text replaced earlier proposals, including one for a 36-hour week and PEC 8/25, which set the same working time over four days. Neither was adopted. The first step, to 42 hours for workers under the CLT labour code, comes two months after promulgation, which Agência Senado dates to 15 December 2026. 40 hours follows 14 months after promulgation, with no pay cut. Workers with a degree and pay of R$ 21,188.88 or more are exempt from the working-hours rules, according to the Chamber's explainer, but keep the two rest days.

A 2024 pilot run by 4 Day Week Brazil tested the 4-day week under 4 Day Week Global's 100-80-100 model (100% of salary for 80% of working hours). Twenty-one companies with about 280 employees began and 19 finished. In the final survey, staff reported weekly hours falling from 42.8 to 36.5, and 71.5% reported higher productivity. The results are self-reported, and the report says revenue gains cannot be credited to the shorter week alone.

## 4-day-week trials in Brazil

What each pilot reported. Figures are the organiser's own, from the linked report.

### 4 Day Week Brazil pilot

Run by 4 Day Week Brazil, Reconnect Happiness at Work; research by FGV-EAESP with 4 Day Week Global partners

- Jan 2024 – Jun 2024
- 21 companies
- 280 employees

Nine-month programme: three months of preparation from September 2023, then six months testing a 4-day week (January to June 2024) at 100% of salary for 80% of working hours. 21 companies (about 280 staff) began and 19 (252 staff) finished. Average weekly hours fell from 42.8 to 36.5. Self-reported survey; 80.2% of staff responded.

71.5% of participants reported higher productivity (a share of respondents, not a size of change). 72.7% of companies reported higher revenue and 63.6% higher profit than in the same period of 2023; the report says this cannot be attributed solely to the 4-day week.

- Mental health: The report states stress fell 14.5% and weekly anxiety 30.5% between December 2023 and July 2024. 72.8% of participants reported less frequent work-related exhaustion (a share of respondents), and 77.3% of participants rated their mental health good to excellent.
- Physical health: 73.7% of participants rated their physical health good to excellent; 43.6% exercised more than three times a week.
- Sleep: Average sleep rose from 6.7 to 7.0 hours a night.

## Working in Brazil

### Working hours

Private-sector employees may normally work no more than 8 hours a day (CLT Article 58). The weekly maximum is 44 hours today. Amendment 141 lowers it to 42 hours two months after promulgation (15 December 2026, according to Agência Senado) and to 40 hours 14 months after promulgation.

### Paid leave and holidays

After each 12 months of employment, an employee is entitled to 30 calendar days of paid leave if they were absent no more than five times in that period (CLT Article 130). Thirty calendar days is four weeks and two days.
