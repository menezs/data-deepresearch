### Outcomes Assessment 

#### Laboratory Alcohol Self-Administration 

Objective self-administration (the primary outcome) was assessed by embedding human laboratory procedures in the trial design. Prior work supports the sensitivity of laboratory selfadministration to detect pharmacotherapy effects.<sup>35,36</sup> A standardized procedure<sup>37</sup> was used to estimate voluntary alcohol consumption and ability to delay drinking.
