# Your Production Schedule for AI Compliance

Key dates you actually need to know. Regulations, guild deadlines, and festival seasons — planned out so you can focus on making your film.

Always current — updated automatically

#### Think of this as your production schedule for compliance.

### Sundance — AI Disclosure Policies Active

Sundance requires filmmakers to disclose any AI tools used in production. Disclosure addendum is part of the submission package.

### EU AI Act — Transparency Obligations (Art. 50)

AI-generated content must be labeled as such for EU audiences. Applies to any film distributed in the EU that uses AI-generated images, video, or text.

### WGA 2026 MBA — Takes Effect

The ratified WGA 2026 Minimum Basic Agreement becomes effective. Companies must notify WGA before licensing scripts for GAI training. Semi-annual AI meetings required.

### Cannes — Festival Season Opens

Cannes has expanded its AI disclosure guidelines. Official Selection now asks for detailed AI usage documentation.

### CA AB 2602 — Consent Enforcement Continues

California's AB 2602 requires meaningful consent for digital replicas. Enforcement is ongoing with increasing scrutiny on AI-generated likenesses.

### SAG-AFTRA 2026 TV/Theatrical Agreement Takes Effect

The ratified 4-year TV/Theatrical Agreement takes effect July 1, 2026 (through June 30, 2030). New requirements include: "articulable business reason" for performer scanning, "significant additional value" standard for synthetic performers, digital replica data security requirements, and arbitration-backed enforcement for violations.

### EU AI Act — High-Risk System Requirements

Extended obligations for high-risk AI systems take effect. While most film AI tools are general-purpose, any biometric or profiling use triggers high-risk classification.

### Sundance — Early Submissions Open

Sundance typically opens early submissions in late summer. Start preparing your AI documentation now for a stress-free submission.

### TIFF — Submission Season

Toronto International Film Festival continues requiring AI disclosure. TIFF's policy aligns with industry-standard documentation.

### Oscar Qualifying Season — AI Disclosure Window

Films seeking Oscar qualification must have complete AI documentation. Academy is expected to formalize AI disclosure requirements.

### WGA — Semi-Annual AI Impact Meeting

First mandatory semi-annual meeting between WGA and signatory companies to discuss AI impact on writers under the 2026 MBA.
