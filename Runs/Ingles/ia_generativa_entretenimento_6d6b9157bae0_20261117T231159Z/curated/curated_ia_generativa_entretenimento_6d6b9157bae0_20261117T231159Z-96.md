### **A.I. is here. We’ve got you covered.** 


SAG-AFTRA is committed to protecting our members' careers in an evolving, tech-driven world. We recognize the dangers of A.I. – and the opportunities it brings to the entertainment industry. 

### Our work to safeguard our members’ livelihoods includes:

#### ***Contract Defense*** 

We negotiate industry-leading contract terms across all work areas to ensure A.I. is used in a way that respects the rights of performers and creators. 


***Legislative Power*** 

We push for state and federal laws that lock in ethical, robust protections for members. 


***Insight & Support*** 

We arm our members with the info they need to better understand A.I. and why union protections matter now more than ever.
