## Is there a 4-day week in Belgium?

Partly. Belgium gives full-time employees a right to request a four-day week, but it is a compressed week: the same weekly hours spread over four longer days, not fewer hours.

Under the law of 3 October 2022, the daily limit can rise to 9.5 hours for a full-time employee who works four days a week. It applies only where the employer's work rules allow it; for weeks over 38 hours a collective agreement is needed. The standard week is 38 hours (the legal limit is 40), so 38 hours fit into four days of 9.5. The employee asks in writing, for a period of up to six months that can be renewed. The employer may refuse but must give written reasons within a month, and the employee cannot be treated unfavourably for asking.

The same reform also lets full-time employees spread their hours over a two-week cycle at their written request, and lengthens the advance notice of variable schedules for part-time workers from five to seven working days.

Genuinely shorter weeks are rare. A state-commissioned pilot run by Ghent University and the Federal Planning Bureau ended in June 2025 with one participating organisation. A federal incentive in force since 2004, lower social-security contributions for cutting collective weekly hours, is often used for extra leave instead.

Caveat: this is a right to ask, not a right to a shorter week, and employers can say no.

## FAQ

**How many companies have a four-day work week?**
About 9% of U.S. organizations had implemented one in SHRM’s 2023 benefits survey, and 8% of U.S. full-time employees said they work four days a week in Gallup’s 2022 data. In Belgium, 2.85% of companies have anyone on the full-time four-day regime (Acerta, 2025), and in the UK, 264 employers are accredited for a permanent four-day week with no loss of pay (4 Day Week Foundation, 2026).

**Does a four-day work week increase productivity?**
The big pilots tracked revenue and self-ratings, not output per hour. UK companies rated the productivity effect 7.5 out of 10 and revenue rose 1.4% over the trial (Autonomy, 2023), and German companies cut about 4 hours a week with no significant change in revenue or profit (2024). **What did the four-day work week trials find?**
Across 141 companies in six countries, burnout, job satisfaction, and mental and physical health improved, with no such pattern in 12 control companies (Nature Human Behaviour, 2025). In the UK, 92% of pilot companies continued and 89% still had it a year later (Autonomy, 2023 and 2024).

**Do employees want a four-day work week?**
81% of U.S. full-time workers and job seekers support one (Bankrate, 2023), and 77% say a four-day, 40-hour week would improve their wellbeing (Bentley-Gallup, 2023). Fewer would pay much for it: Owl Labs found workers would give up 8% of salary (2025), while Portugal’s pilot workers valued theirs at 28% (2024).

# Four-day working week? Eight out of ten SMEs not behind the idea

Last week, parliament gave the go-ahead for the labour deal. One of the most notable measures is **the** **four-day working week**: full-time workers are given the option of spreading working time over four days instead of the usual five. This offers full-time employees with an additional day off. Note: **as an employer, you are not obliged to introduce the four-day working week**.

## Full-time working week in four days

Performing a full-time job in four longer days a week instead of five shorter ones. The labour deal will allow employees to request this from their employer, provided the option is provided in the company. If the employer agrees to the employee’s request, a written agreement will be concluded.

## Legal implications four-day working week labour deal

Introducing a four-day working week obviously means that the normal working hours of up to eight hours a day cannot be sustained. The employee will have to perform more hours in one working day to achieve full-time employment in four days. **This deviation must be included either in the labour regulations or in a collective agreement**:

**1. Weekly working hours ≤ 38 hours**

If the employee has full-time employment of up to 38 hours a week, the daily working hours can be increased from eight to a maximum of 9.5 hours through the labour regulations.

**2. Weekly working hours > 38 hours (maximum 40 hours)**

If weekly working hours exceed 38, daily working hours can only be increased by means of a collective agreement. For instance, a 40-hour working week can be performed at the rate of four 10-hour days after a collective agreement has been concluded.

Moreover, an employee who receives permission from the employer to perform a four-day working week retains the **right to all statutory holidays**. Voluntary **overtime is prohibited** in this system on days not worked. The aim of this scheme is to achieve a better work-life balance, thereby also preventing burnout.
